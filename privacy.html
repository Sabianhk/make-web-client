Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, you choose which access to grant. The app may then access:

  • your basic account identity (email address), used only to confirm which account is signed in;
  • data in the Google services you authorize, such as Google Tag Manager containers you already have access to, and, where granted, Google Drive or Google Sheets files.

How the data is used

The data is used only to perform the actions the signed-in user asks for, such as reading or updating their own Tag Manager configuration. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Google user data is not sold, transferred, or shared with third parties.

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
